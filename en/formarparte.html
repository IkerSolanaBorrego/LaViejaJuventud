Why become a volunteer?

Because behind every visit, every call and every workshop there is an elderly person who feels accompanied again.
Share your time with those who need it most.




🤝 Company

Visit elderly people in their homes, go for a walk with them or simply chat for a while. Your presence means everything.

🎓 Workshops

Do you know about technology, cooking, crafts or languages? Share your knowledge in our weekly workshops.

📢 Outreach

Help us grow on social media, at events or by telling your friends and family about what we do.
