Who are we?

La Vieja Juventud is a foundation that seeks to reduce loneliness among older adults, with the goal of improving their emotional and social well-being.


Get to know us

What do we do?

We combat loneliness with in-person and telephone support. We also organize activities to socialize and improve their mobility.
