Who are we?

Our Story

La Vieja Juventud was born in 2026 when a group of SMX2 students from the JoanXXIII center noticed that many elderly neighbors were living completely alone. Since then, we have been working to reduce loneliness through companionship, phone calls, and social activities.



🎯 Mission

To improve the quality of life of all elderly people who, for any unwanted reason, cannot carry out their daily activities normally or whose emotional well-being is not adequate.

🏆 Aspiration

To ensure that every elderly person can live their later years with fulfillment, safety, and the certainty of having someone by their side.

❤️ Commitment

To accompany every day, listen to every story, and guarantee that no elderly person feels alone or invisible in our community.
