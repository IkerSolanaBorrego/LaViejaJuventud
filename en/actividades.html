Activities

Bingo Afternoon

In this activity, volunteers spend the afternoon with the elderly playing bingo. While they play, they talk, laugh, and spend time together so the elderly feel accompanied and happy. Besides having fun, this activity helps them exercise their memory and attention.

Imserso Trip

During the Imserso trips, volunteers accompany the elderly on excursions and visits to different places. They help them with anything they need and spend time with them so they feel safe and supported. Thanks to these outings, older people can discover new places, break their routine, and enjoy beautiful experiences with others.

Painting Workshop

In the painting workshop, volunteers help the elderly paint, draw, and do crafts. Together they create drawings and share a calm and fun time. This activity helps them relax, express their ideas, and enjoy spending time together.

Community Garden

In this activity, volunteers and seniors work together in a small community garden. Together they plant tomatoes and herbs, take turns watering the plants, and harvest the crops as a group. This activity allows them to spend time outdoors, work as a team, and enjoy nature.

Team Cooking

In team cooking, volunteers cook various recipes with the seniors, such as pizzas, cookies, omelets, or traditional dishes. While preparing the food, they talk, share experiences, and enjoy a fun and pleasant moment in each other's company.

Exercise Class

In yoga sessions, volunteers help seniors perform gentle exercises, breathing techniques, and stretches with relaxing music. They also practice chair yoga so that everyone can participate comfortably and safely. This activity helps them relax, improve mobility, and feel better both physically and emotionally.

Sewing Workshop

In the sewing workshop, volunteers spend time with the elderly while sewing and knitting different things such as: blankets, sweaters, towels... This helps them relax and make things they can give as gifts to their grandchildren or family members.
